ShadowBench: Toward Reliable Automatic Evaluation of Semantic Alignment in Autoformalization
Hojae Han*, Jongyoon Kim*, Sanghyeok Park*, Dongwook Cheon, Yeachan Park, Myeong Jae Jeon, Sunjong Choe, Soonho Kong, Wonseok Hur, Seung-won Hwang†, Donghoon Hyeon†
EMNLP 2026 · arXiv:2608.29270 · * equal contribution, † corresponding authors

Introduces SA-Pass, which scores a formal statement by Lean-checked implications to and from hidden auxiliary theorems (shadows), and ShadowBench, 178 postgraduate- to research-level Lean 4 problems. SA-Pass agrees with expert judgments on 98.8% of cases; the best system in the paper compiles 61.8% but reaches 11.2% SA-Pass.

Formalizing Faugère's F4 Algorithm in Lean 4
Dongwook Cheon
Manuscript · October 2026

A Lean 4 formalization of Faugère's F4 algorithm over Mathlib's multivariate polynomials. For every finite set \(F\) of nonzero polynomials over a field in finitely many variables, the procedure terminates and returns a finite set \(G\) with \(\langle G\rangle = \langle F\rangle\) and \(\langle \mathrm{LM}(g) : g \in G\rangle = \mathrm{in}_\prec(\langle F\rangle)\), so \(G\) is a Gröbner basis (theorem F4_groebner). Symbolic preprocessing and Gaussian elimination work directly on finite sets of polynomials; termination uses a lexicographic well-founded relation. The checked results use only Lean's three standard axioms, with no sorry.
