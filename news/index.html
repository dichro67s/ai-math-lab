News

Announcements, releases and results.

  • 2026-11Lab website relaunched with our benchmark, leaderboard and publications.
  • 2026-11Lean4All is online: an open platform for formalizing mathematics in Lean 4 with AI agents. It works through GitHub, offers blueprints, and sets up missions from an arXiv paper or an uploaded file; Lean checks every proof.
  • 2026-10-26New manuscript: Dongwook Cheon, Formalizing Faugère's F4 Algorithm in Lean 4. The formalized F4 procedure provably terminates and returns a Gröbner basis of the input ideal. Code.
  • 2026-10-20Leaderboard update. New agentic results on ShadowBench: Codex (GPT-6.1 Sol) with Numina-Lean-Agent reaches 18.5% SA-Pass, the best so far. Claude Code (Opus 5.5) with Numina-Lean-Agent compiles 93.8% of its outputs, yet only 16.9% pass SA-Pass.
  • 2026-10-14Leaderboard update. Frontier models prompted directly (Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Fable 5.1; GPT-6 Sol, Luna, Astra) compile up to 38.2% of their outputs but reach at most 1.1% SA-Pass.
  • 2026-09ShadowBench will appear at EMNLP 2026.
  • 2026-08-29ShadowBench preprint posted on arXiv (2608.29270), with code, the public dataset and a public leaderboard.
  • 2026-04 – 06An early 126-problem version of ShadowBench was Track 4 of the ICML 2026 AI for Math Workshop & Challenge: end-to-end autoformalization and proving in Lean 4. About 117 teams registered and 27 took part.
